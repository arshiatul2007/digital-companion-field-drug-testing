What is Digital Companion?

The system helps convert a visual field-test process into a structured digital workflow.

Traditional colorimetric field-test kits rely on visual interpretation of a colour-change reaction. This can make results subjective and may leave limited digital evidence about when and where a test was conducted.

Digital Companion works alongside existing field-test kits. A device camera captures the test image together with a reference colour card. The application then processes the image and creates a digital record containing test information, timestamp, location and cryptographic hashes.

How It Works

A simple four-step digital workflow.

1

Capture

Capture the test result and reference colour card.

2

Analyse

The system processes the captured image and generates a demonstration result.

3

Record

Timestamp, operator information and location are stored.

4

Verify

Cryptographic hashes and signatures help detect record changes.

Important Note

⚠️ Prototype / Presumptive Result

This prototype demonstrates the digital workflow and image-processing concept. The demonstration colour classifier is not a validated method for identifying drugs and must not replace laboratory confirmatory testing.

A production system would require validated interpretation criteria for the specific commercial field-test kit, colour calibration, quality checks and appropriate security controls.
